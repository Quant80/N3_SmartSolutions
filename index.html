∑ Mathematics
Quadratic Formula
$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$$
Compound Interest / Depreciation
$$A = P(1+i)^n \quad A = P(1-i)^n$$
Arithmetic Sequence & Series
$$T_n = a+(n-1)d \quad S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a+(n-1)d]$$
Geometric Sequence & Series
$$T_n = ar^{n-1} \quad S_\infty = \frac{a}{1-r}, \;|r|<1$$
First Principles Derivative
$$f'(x) = \lim_{h\to 0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$$
Compound Angles
$$\sin(\alpha\pm\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta\pm\cos\alpha\sin\beta$$
Sine & Cosine Rules
$$\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B} \quad a^2=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A$$
⚡ Physical Sciences — Paper 1 (Physics)
Newton's 2nd Law
$$\vec{F}_{net} = m\vec{a}$$
Kinematics
$$v=u+at \quad v^2=u^2+2as \quad s=ut+\tfrac{1}{2}at^2$$
Work, Energy, Power
$$W=Fs\cos\theta \quad E_k=\tfrac{1}{2}mv^2 \quad P=\frac{W}{\Delta t}$$
Waves & Doppler
$$v=f\lambda \quad f_L=\frac{v\pm v_L}{v\pm v_s}f_s$$
Electricity (Ohm's Law)
$$V=IR \quad P=VI=I^2R=\frac{V^2}{R} \quad Q=It$$
Coulomb's Law
$$F=\frac{kQ_1Q_2}{r^2} \quad E=\frac{kQ}{r^2}$$
⚗ Physical Sciences — Paper 2 (Chemistry)
Moles
$$n=\frac{m}{M} \quad n=cV \quad n=\frac{V}{22.4}$$
Ideal Gas Law
$$pV=nRT \quad R=8.314 \text{ J·mol}^{-1}\text{·K}^{-1}$$
Electrochemistry
$$E°_{cell} = E°_{cathode} - E°_{anode}$$
